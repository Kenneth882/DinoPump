# DinoPump

DinoPump is a shared fictional market game. This glossary defines the language
used for its Daily Roar news and narration.

## Language

**Authored news**:
The prepared Daily Roar account of a fictional market event and its recorded
effects. It provides the complete event information when AI narration is absent.

**AI narration**:
Optional Daily Roar commentary connecting a recorded market event to affected
assets' prices and aggregate executed activity, in a clear newsreader voice with
light dinosaur humor. It describes recorded facts and has no authority over
events, trades, resources or results.
_Avoid_: AI analyst, trading adviser

**Narration context**:
The fixed account of affected assets and aggregate executed activity available
when a source event happens, covering activity since the previous scheduled event
or round opening. It describes that historical moment even when narration arrives
later.
